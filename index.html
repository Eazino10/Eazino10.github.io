01. About Me

Hello! I'm Olusegun Israel Olajire, a 300-level Computer Engineering student at the University of Lagos (UNILAG) with a strong passion for understanding how systems work from the silicon up to the software.

My focus lies heavily in embedded systems, where I design multi-microcontroller networks and audio amplification circuits. I also have a keen interest in financial technologies, specifically developing automated algorithmic trading scripts using Pine Script and MQL5.

I'm a methodical problem-solver always looking for opportunities to apply my technical proficiency in C++, microcontrollers, and system architecture to real-world challenges.
